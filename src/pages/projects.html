Erasmus+ Project

YOUTHCENTS – Financial Literacy for Youth

YOUTHCENTS is a European project designed to empower young people aged 15–26 with essential financial literacy and entrepreneurship skills.

  • Financial literacy and practical money-management skills
  • Entrepreneurship and future planning for young people
  • Digital learning tools and non-formal education methods
  • Support for disadvantaged and neurodiverse learners

Led by partners in Portugal, Malta, and Poland, YOUTHCENTS promotes financial inclusion, sustainable thinking, and youth employability across Europe.

Project Initiative

SPARK – Sharing Practical Innovation and Learning Tools

SPARK focuses on sharing practical learning approaches, tools, and resources that support youth engagement, innovation, and accessible education.

  • Exchange of practical tools and learning resources
  • Innovation in youth education and engagement
  • Accessible approaches for diverse learners and communities
  • Collaboration and knowledge-sharing across partners

SPARK strengthens collaboration and helps make innovative learning methods more visible, transferable, and useful across different contexts.
