Who We Are

youTHrive is a forward-thinking organisation dedicated to empowering people by unlocking their potential through innovative learning experiences. With extensive training experience, we understand the importance of tailored approaches that respond to diverse needs and disadvantaged backgrounds.

Our programmes combine engaging methodologies such as animated content, vibrant visuals, gamification, and practical learning tools to make learning accessible, enjoyable, and relevant to real-world challenges.

Our Leadership Team

Meet the people driving our mission forward

Kevin Spiteri

Managing Director

Kevin Spiteri has extensive experience in training youth and adult workers, course evaluation, and educational consultancy. He specialises in addressing the challenges faced by educators and young learners as they adapt to the digital age.

Kevin brings expertise in developing training tools and professional development programmes for adult trainers, as well as evaluation techniques that strengthen didactical and transversal skills. His open mindset and deep understanding of training tools support youth workers in developing essential skills for the evolving labour market. He is committed to promoting inclusiveness and sharing best practices that help trainers cultivate meaningful and future-ready competences.

Marika Huber

Managing Director

Marika Huber brings extensive experience in European project management, leadership, and financial management, particularly within the Erasmus programme.

She has worked on projects related to women’s empowerment in the workplace and currently contributes to Erasmus adult projects focused on equality. In her previous role as Managing Director of the Mediterranean Bank Network, she built strong sector relationships that supported social entrepreneurship and women’s entrepreneurship. As a Director at the Malta Institute of Management, she contributed directly to the professional development of trainers and organisations. Marika holds an MBA from Maastricht.
